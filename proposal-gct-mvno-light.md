## 1. Executive summary

GCT Group can enter the Nigerian mobile market without building an MVNO of its own. This proposal covers one option only, the MVNO Light (Digital Mobile) model, together with the full range of value added services that can be sold on top of it.

Under MVNO Light, GCT Group sells mobile service under the GCT brand. Digital Mobile, a DSG Group operator, provides the MVNO licence position, the platform, the network interconnect and the day to day operations. GCT Group provides the brand, the customer base and the sales channel. The two parties share the net profit equally.

The commercial shape is a single set up fee of **$20,000**, a monthly platform and management fee of **$5,000 or $0.48 per active subscriber, whichever is higher**, and a **50/50** split of net profit per subscriber. There is no capital expenditure and no licence application.

Three points define this model:

1. GCT Group carries no platform capital cost and no regulatory licence obligation.
2. Launch takes about 16 weeks from contract signature, against 9 to 12 months for a full MVNO.
3. Every value added service in Section 6 can be attached at launch or later, each with its own revenue share.

This proposal replaces neither the DBOT transformation proposal of 19 January 2026 nor the full MVNO proposal of 28 January 2026. It sets out the light option on its own so GCT Group can price and sign it separately.

## 3. What MVNO Light is

MVNO Light is a profit share arrangement over an existing MVNO. GCT Group does not hold the mobile licence, does not own the platform and does not carry the network agreements. Digital Mobile holds all three and configures its platform to run the GCT Group brand as a separate operation.

The model is prepaid and digital first. Subscribers join through the GCT Group website or app, verify their identity digitally, receive an eSIM or a physical SIM, and recharge through digital channels.

### 3.1 How the two models compare

| Dimension | MVNO Light (this proposal) | Full MVNO |
|-----------|---------------------------|-----------|
| Licence held by | Digital Mobile | GCT Group |
| Platform owned by | Digital Mobile | GCT Group, licenced from MVNE |
| Set up fee | $20,000 | Per the 28 January 2026 proposal |
| Time to launch | About 16 weeks | 9 to 12 months |
| Capital expenditure | None | Platform, integration, licence costs |
| Billing types | Prepaid only | Prepaid, subscription and postpaid |
| International roaming | Not included | Included |
| Product control | Predefined bundles, limited customisation | Full catalogue control |
| Commercial model | 50/50 net profit share | GCT Group keeps margin, pays platform fees |
| Regulatory obligation | Digital Mobile | GCT Group |

MVNO Light suits a first market entry, a digital only sub brand, or a test of demand before committing to a full MVNO. Each brand in the GCT Group could run its own MVNO Light operation on the same platform.

## 4. Why MVNO Light for GCT Group

GCT Group is one of Nigeria's leading FTTH and enterprise connectivity providers. That existing base is the reason this model works.

**Mobile completes the bundle.** A fibre customer who also takes GCT Group mobile is materially harder for a competitor to win. Cross selling mobile into the installed fibre base costs a fraction of acquiring a new mobile subscriber cold.

**The channel already exists.** GCT Group has customers, billing relationships, a brand and a sales team. MVNO Light supplies the only missing piece, which is the mobile network capability.

**The downside is capped.** The exposure is the $20,000 set up fee and the monthly minimum of $5,000. If the market does not respond, the loss is measured in tens of thousands of dollars rather than millions.

**Value added services carry the margin.** Mobile voice and data are low margin in Nigeria. The Bitdefender rate card in Section 6.4 is priced at 2.5 times wholesale cost, so gross profit is 60% of the retail price on every package. The mobile subscription earns the customer relationship, and the value added services earn the profit.

## 5. Scope of work

The platform capability below is taken from Section 7.2 of the MVNE MVNO proposal of 28 January 2026 and localised for Nigeria.

### 5.1 SIM lifecycle management and provisioning

- eSIM first. Digital onboarding and provisioning, with physical SIMs available as an option.
- Bulk and individual provisioning through digital channels.
- Churn management, inventory control and SIM security, streamlined for a digital operation.

### 5.2 Account management

- Self service account creation, upgrade and closure.
- Multi SIM and account hierarchy, simplified for digital use.

### 5.3 Product and bundling management

- Predefined voice, data and SMS bundles. Customisation is limited by design, which is what keeps the cost down.
- Promotions and loyalty mechanics through digital channels.

### 5.4 Recharge and activity management

- Digital recharge through app, web and USSD.
- Real time balance enquiry through digital self service.

### 5.5 Events and notifications

- SMS, email and push notification for every key subscriber event.

### 5.6 Core value added services platform

- SMS, voicemail, USSD and WhatsApp included in the platform.
- APIs available for digital partners.

### 5.7 Billing and financial management

- Prepaid only. Subscription and postpaid billing are not included in this model.
- Automated invoicing and statements, delivered digitally.

### 5.8 Regulatory and compliance, Nigeria

- SIM registration in line with the Nigerian Communications Commission SIM Registration Regulations.
- National Identity Number verification through NIMC integration.
- Compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.
- Number portability and lawful intercept supported.

Digital Mobile carries the regulatory obligation as the licence holder. GCT Group carries the obligation to present accurate subscriber information at the point of sale.

### 5.9 CRM and administration

- Digital CRM with real time analytics, segmentation and campaign management.

### 5.10 OSS and BSS

- Real time charging, digital product catalogue and operational dashboards.

### 5.11 Support and service desk

- Digital first support through chat, email and a knowledge base.
- Escalation to second and third line support as required.
- Voice contact centre delivered by CXG and managed by Digital Mobile.

### 5.12 Portal management

- Digital self service portal for mobile and web, role based, white labelled to the GCT Group brand.

### 5.13 Integration

- APIs for digital partners.
- Zero rating supported for GCT Group digital properties.

### 5.14 Exclusions

The following sit outside this proposal:

- Hardware procurement.
- Marketing spend and physical retail estate.
- Postpaid billing and international roaming.
- Handset financing and device sales.

## 6. Value added services

Every service below can be attached to the MVNO Light operation. Each is priced separately and each can be added at launch or at any point afterwards.

### 6.1 Included in the platform at no extra cost

| Service | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| SMS | Person to person and application to person messaging |
| Voicemail | Standard voicemail with notification |
| USSD | Menu driven self service for balance, recharge and bundle purchase |
| WhatsApp | Business channel for support and self service |
| Digital notifications | SMS, email and push for all subscriber events |

### 6.2 MVNO PWA platform, subscriber self service app

A branded progressive web app giving subscribers SIM management, top ups, bundle purchase, account management and support. Installs from the browser with no app store dependency.

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Set up and development, once off | $12,507 |
| Monthly support and development | $4,250 |

The monthly fee covers 40 hours of tier 3 support at $3,250 and 10 hours of development at $1,000.

### 6.3 Travel eSIM

A travel data proposition sold to GCT Group subscribers and to the wider Nigerian outbound travel market. Subscribers buy a destination data bundle before departure and activate it on arrival.

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Platform configuration, once off | $3,200 |
| SIM profile creation, per subscriber | $0.63 |
| ICCID provisioning, per subscriber | $0.31 |
| Active IMSI, per subscriber per month | $0.31 |
| Margin share on every bundle sold | 50/50 |

Integration takes about two weeks.

### 6.4 Bitdefender cybersecurity

Consumer security sold as a monthly subscription and billed to the subscriber's mobile account. Delivered by the Digital Resilience division of DSG. No additional infrastructure is required.

A set up fee of **$2,000** applies once. Revenue share is **50/50** between GCT Group and DSG on all subscriber billings.

**Bitdefender Security with Digital Identity Protection**

| 1 device | $1.08 | $2.70 | $1.62 |
| 5 devices | $2.38 | $5.96 | $3.58 |
| 10 devices | $3.40 | $8.50 | $5.10 |

**Bitdefender Security with Dark Web Monitoring and VPN**

| Package | Wholesale cost | Retail price | Gross profit |
|---------|---------------|--------------|--------------|
| 1 device | $1.84 | $4.60 | $2.76 |
| 5 devices | $3.14 | $7.86 | $4.72 |
| 10 devices | $5.22 | $13.06 | $7.84 |

All prices are per subscriber per month in US dollars. Retail prices are the recommended starting point for the GCT Group subscriber tariff. Gross profit is split 50/50 under the revenue share.

### 6.5 Xanite CVM platform

The BroadBrand Customer Value Management platform, built for mobile operators. Xanite runs campaign automation and segmentation, predicts churn and triggers retention offers, and lifts ARPU through targeted upsell. Campaign delivery covers SMS, push, email and USSD.

Licenced on a platform fee scaled to subscriber base size and active campaign volume. Integration with the MVNO platform is included in the set up scope. Price on request following a scoping session.

### 6.6 PersAIc personalisation engine

An AI personalisation engine serving real time offers, bundle recommendations and content across the app, the web and messaging channels. Includes predictive lifetime value and next best action modelling. Designed to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023.

Priced per active subscriber. Implementation and integration with the MVNO platform and the PWA are included in onboarding. Price on request.

### 6.7 CXG omni channel customer experience

Voice, email, chat and social customer service, WhatsApp automation, social media management, CRM implementation and subscriber analytics.

Under MVNO Light the voice contact centre is delivered by CXG and managed by Digital Mobile, and its cost sits inside the net profit calculation in Section 7. Additional CXG scope beyond the standard service is priced on request against volume, channel mix and service levels.

### 6.8 MARS, mediation, assurance and revenue services

Revenue protection for the operation. MARS covers five domains:

| Domain | Function |
|--------|----------|
| Mediation | Processes raw EDR, GDR and CDR files into usable data, with enrichment and real time ingestion |
| Assurance | Audits systems against each other, validates service reliability, monitors continuously |
| Revenue | Detects and corrects revenue leakage in real time, tracks revenue flows end to end |
| Service enablers | Task automation, workflows, reporting services and machine learning scheduling |
| Enablers | Delivers accuracy, operational efficiency and sustained margin |

MARS also validates wholesale bills, which matters when the host network invoice is the single largest cost line in the operation.

Priced on data volumes, integration complexity and the module combination required. Price on request following a requirements session.

### 6.9 VMS, voucher management server

End to end lifecycle management of prepaid airtime, data and promotional vouchers. Covers generation, distribution through retail, agent, WhatsApp and USSD channels, real time redemption validation, and expiry and reactivation handling. Full integration with the MVNO platform.

VMS matters in Nigeria because a large share of prepaid value still moves through physical agent and dealer networks. Priced on voucher volume, channel complexity and integration scope. Price on request.

## 7. Commercial terms

### 7.1 Once off fees

| Item | Amount | When payable |
|------|--------|--------------|
| Project set up fee | $20,000 | On contract signature, in advance |

There are no other upfront costs from either party.

### 7.2 Who pays for what at set up

| Item | GCT Group | Digital Mobile | Note |
|------|-----------|----------------|------|
| Web development | Yes | | GCT Group covers changes to its own website, or Digital Mobile can provide a white labelled site |
| MVNO platform deployment | | Yes | Covered under existing Digital Mobile infrastructure cost |

### 7.3 Monthly and recurring fees

| Item | GCT Group | Digital Mobile | Charge |
|------|-----------|----------------|--------|
| Platform and management fee | | Yes | $5,000 per month, or $0.48 per active subscriber, whichever is higher |
| Contact centre | | Yes | Run by CXG, managed by Digital Mobile |
| Airtime recharge distribution | | Yes | Through Digital Mobile distribution channels |
| SIM cards | | Yes | $0.65 per SIM |
| Digital SIM registration, NIN verification | | Yes | $0.45 per SIM |
| SIM delivery and logistics | | Yes | $2.15 per delivery, where applicable |

Airtime distribution costs 7% when recharge runs through a third party channel. It costs nothing when recharge runs through the GCT Group website or app, which is a direct reason to drive subscribers to owned channels.

The per SIM, registration and logistics figures were set in South African rand in the 28 January 2026 proposal at R12, R8 and R40. The US dollar figures above are indicative conversions at roughly R18.50 to the dollar. **These three figures need to be fixed in the contract, since the underlying rand rate card and the exchange rate both move.** The platform fee of $5,000 or $0.48 per subscriber was quoted in US dollars in the source proposal and needs no conversion.

### 7.4 Profit share

Net profit per subscriber is split **50/50** between GCT Group and Digital Mobile.

Net profit is ARPU less the wholesale network cost, less the Digital Mobile platform fee, less contact centre cost where a contact is made, and less airtime distribution cost where a third party channel is used.

Driving recharge through GCT Group owned digital channels removes the N616 distribution cost and lifts the GCT Group share by about N308 per subscriber per month.
