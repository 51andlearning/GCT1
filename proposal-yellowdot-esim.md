## Commercial Terms

The costs are structured around the configuration and operating of the Travel eSIM platform for Yellow Dot. There is a **once-off project set-up fee** paid upfront, and then a **revenue share** arrangement.

### Project Fee

| Item | Fee (excl. VAT) |
|------|----------------|
| Platform configuration (once-off) | **$3,200** |

### Revenue Share

A **50/50 split on margin** per bundle sold (see Addendum 1 for detail).

### Per-Subscriber Fees

All created bundles must include the following platform fees charged to Yellow Dot:

| Fee Item | Rate |
|----------|------|
| One-time SIM profile creation fee | $0.63 per subscriber |
| One-time ICCID provisioning fee | $0.31 per ICCID |
| Monthly active IMSI Credential fee | $0.31 per active IMSI/month |

> **Note:** ICCID provisioning fees are invoiced to Yellow Dot at the time each ICCID is provisioned on the Connectivity Hub platform. Monthly active IMSI fees are based on IMSI Credentials that had a data session, SMS event, or location update in that month.
>
> *MVNE has 0–250,000 Profile Credentials ordered.*
