## Introduction

This proposal outlines the terms for the **build and support for the Yellow Dot MVNO PWA platform** and monthly development hours.

It outlines a support requirement for the new platform as well as the **24×7 support** provided for critical issues, and development hours for platform changes.

---

## Scope of Work

Support of the developed functionality and integration to the **Yellow Dot MVNO platform**.

**Support Level:** Level 3 support will be provided.

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Level 1 | Monitoring of the system functionality *(can be added)* |
| Level 2 | System functional issues that can be resolved by restarting or manual intervention |
| **Level 3** | **Code-level corrections required** *(provided by MVNE)* |

**Additional services include:**
- Identification of features, additions, and corrections — change request-based, billed on time and material basis
- Support issues logged with the support team following the SLA defined in the SOW between Yellow Dot and MVNE
- Monthly development hours, which may be **accumulated up to a maximum of three months**
