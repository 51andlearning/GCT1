## GCT Group DBOT Summary

GCT Group is one of Nigeria's leading FTTH/enterprise connectivity providers. DSG proposes a **Design-Build-Operate-Transfer (DBOT)** engagement to transform GCT into Nigeria's first fully converged, cloud-enabled digital telco.

### DBOT Phases

| Phase | Duration | Description |
|-------|----------|-------------|
| Design | 8 weeks | Strategy review, BRS, business model, GTM |
| Build & Operate | 6 months | Transformation Project Office, implementation |
| Transfer | 1 month | Handover, governance, go-independent certification |

**Investment:** USD 250,000 (fixed fee) + travel & accommodation + $300/hr additional consulting

### Payment Structure
- 50% deposit on acceptance
- Balance monthly as defined

## Yellow Dot PWA Support Summary

- **Once-off:** $12,507 — PWA set up and development
- **Monthly:** $4,250 ($3,250 for 40 hrs tier 3 support + $1,000 for 10 hrs development)
- Invoicing: Monthly, due in MVNE's bank account by the 25th of each month

## Yellow Dot Travel eSIM Summary

- **Once-off:** $3,200 — platform configuration
- **Revenue share:** 50/50 split on margin per bundle sold
- **Per-subscriber fees:** $0.63 SIM profile creation, $0.31 ICCID provisioning, $0.31/month active IMSI
- **Timeline:** ~2 weeks for full integration
