## Executive Summary

GCT Group stands at a pivotal inflection point — transitioning from a traditional fibre ISP to **Nigeria's first fully converged, cloud-enabled, customer-centric digital telecoms service provider**.

DSG proposes a **Design-Build-Operate-Transfer (DBOT)** partnership that delivers measurable outcomes:

- **Sustainable revenue growth** through diversified product innovation
- **Operational efficiency** through automation and digital process redesign
- **Customer excellence** through data-driven engagement and omnichannel experience

This partnership will create a **future-ready GCT Group MVNO** — financially strong, digitally agile, and strategically positioned to lead Nigeria's telecom renaissance.

## Background and Context

### Executive Overview

GCT Group has built a formidable reputation as one of Nigeria's leading **fibre-to-the-home (FTTH)**, enterprise connectivity, and data centre service providers. With a strong presence in Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, GCT Group is a premium connectivity brand trusted by residential and enterprise customers.

The next strategic evolution is the addition of a **Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO)** capability — enabling fully integrated fixed-mobile convergence (FMC) services under the GCT Group brand.

### Why an MVNO Makes Strategic Sense

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Fixed-Mobile Convergence | Bundled Fibre + Mobile, shared data pools, unified billing |
| Enterprise & SME Upsell | Corporate mobile fleets, IoT SIMs, M2M, LTE failover |
| Residential Cross-Sell | Value-based mobile bundles, family SIM packs, loyalty rewards |
| Asset-Light Expansion | No spectrum/RAN investment; leverage existing MNO networks |

### Nigeria Market Context

- 200+ million mobile subscriptions
- Data consumption growing double digits annually
- Increasing demand for bundled connectivity
- Growing SME digitisation

### Proposed MVNO Positioning

**Brand Concept:** *"GCT Group One Network — Seamless Home, Office & Mobile Connectivity"*

**Core Pillars:**
- Converged connectivity
- Premium customer experience
- Enterprise-grade reliability
- Transparent pricing
- Data-led loyalty rewards
- Value-added services — Travel eSIM, Cyber Security (Bit Defender)

### Competitive Differentiation

| Traditional MNO | GCT Group MVNO Advantage |
|----------------|--------------------------|
| Mobile-only | Fixed + Mobile convergence |
| Mass market | Premium urban segment |
| Generic bundles | Converged smart home bundles |
| Limited enterprise integration | Full ICT + fibre + mobile integration |

## Transfer Phase

The Transfer Phase transitions full operational control from the MVNE team to GCT Group's internal management.

**Duration:** 1 month

### Success Indicators

| Indicator | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Operational independence | 100% of systems managed internally |
| Commercial performance | Agreed KPIs delivered over 90 days |
| Leadership capability | Full operational confidence by GCT Group management |

### Transfer Framework Streams

| Stream | Activities |
|--------|-----------|
| Operational Readiness | System audits, health checks, SLA/KPI validation over 90-day window |
| Knowledge & Skills Transfer | Executive workshops, functional shadowing, technical knowledge transfer, Transfer Playbook |
| Documentation & Governance | Architecture diagrams, SOPs, SLAs, ownership records, governance structure |
| Performance Benchmarking | ARPU, churn, uptime, activation, CSAT metrics — joint acceptance sign-off |
| Exit & Support Assurance | 3–6 month post-transfer support, escalation channels, "Go-Independent" certification |

### Transfer Roadmap

| Phase | Duration | Key Activities | Deliverables |
|-------|----------|----------------|--------------|
| T1 – Preparation & Planning | Week 1 | Define scope, finalise audit, agree success metrics | Transfer Plan, Acceptance Criteria |
| T2 – Knowledge Transfer & Training | Week 2 | Workshops, job shadowing, system walk-throughs | Training Completion Reports |
| T3 – Operational Validation | Week 3 | Live ops under supervision, monitor SLAs | Readiness Dashboard, KPI Validation |
| T4 – Handover & Certification | Week 4 | Final handover, documentation transfer, governance activation | Handover Certificates, Support MoU |

*A Joint Transfer Steering Committee — co-chaired by the GCT Group CEO and DSG CEO — will oversee the transition.*
